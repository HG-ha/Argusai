ArgusAI Security Workbench

A local desktop app for authorized security review of web apps, mobile
packages, and native binaries. It includes an AI assistant, a passive
proxy, a reverse workbench, checks, and reports.

ArgusAI is not “a scanner with a chat box”. Work can be planned, tool-
driven, confirmed, archived, and resumed. Data stays on this machine;
secrets are not uploaded to an ArgusAI server.

Scope
- Use it only on sites and samples you own, or targets you are written-
  authorized to test.
- Probes are non-destructive by default. Do not use it for unauthorized
  scanning, credential stuffing, or destructive payloads.
- Do not send requests outside the declared scope.

After install
- Launch from the Start Menu or desktop shortcut.
- Start-at-logon can be enabled in Settings and also appears under
  Windows Settings → Apps → Startup.
- Uninstall from the Start Menu shortcut.
