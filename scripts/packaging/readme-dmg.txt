ArgusAI Security Workbench / 安全工作台

Drag ArgusAI to Applications, then open it from Launchpad or Spotlight.
把 ArgusAI 拖到 Applications，再从启动台或 Spotlight 打开。

What it is
A local desktop workbench for authorized security review: AI assistant,
passive proxy, reverse tools, checks, and reports. Data stays on this Mac.

这是什么
本机安全工作台：AI 助手、被动代理、逆向工具、检查与报告。数据保存在这台 Mac。

Scope / 使用范围
Use only on sites and samples you own, or targets you are written-
authorized to test. Do not scan out of scope.
只用于你自己的站点、样本，或已获得书面授权的目标。不要扫范围外主机。
